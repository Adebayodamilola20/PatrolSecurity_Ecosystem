TARMAC SECURITYGuard Operations app

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Tarmac Security mobile app (Android package ng.tarmacsecurity.patrol) and the Tarmac Security web portals collect, why, and how it is protected. The app is operated by Tarmac Security Ltd ("Tarmac", "we") for its own guards and supervisors. It is a work tool: accounts are created by Tarmac, and it is not intended for the general public.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, work email, phone number, role, and a profile photo if Tarmac adds one.
  • Location: precise GPS location
    • when you clock in and clock out (to confirm you are at your post),
    • when you scan a checkpoint, and
    • about every 30 seconds while you are on duty, so the control room can see where guards are and send help to the right place.
    Location is only shared while you are clocked in, and a notification ("On duty — location shared") is shown the whole time. It stops when you clock out. The app does not collect your location when you are off duty.
  • Camera and photos: the camera is used to scan checkpoint QR codes (the camera image is not stored) and to take photos you choose to attach, such as clock-in/clock-out photos and incident or maintenance photos.
  • Work records: shifts, checkpoint scans, reports, observations, post-order acknowledgements and emergency alerts you submit.
  • Device check: whether the phone reports a simulated ("mock") location, so fake GPS cannot be used to clock in.

2. Why we use it

  • To run patrols and confirm guards are at their assigned sites.
  • To keep guards safe: emergency alerts and knowing where help is needed.
  • To produce patrol, attendance and incident reports for Tarmac and for the client whose site you protect.
  • To keep accounts secure (for example, locking an account after repeated wrong passwords).

We do not sell your information, show advertising, or use it for advertising.

3. Who can see it

  • Tarmac staff with the right role (administrators and supervisors).
  • Tarmac's clients can see patrol and incident records for their own sites only.
  • Service providers that host the system for us under contract: Convex (database and file storage, hosted in Europe) and Vercel (web portals). They process data only to provide the service.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

  • The live location trail recorded during shifts is deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Shift, scan, report and incident records are kept as long as Tarmac needs them for its operations, client contracts and legal obligations.
  • When you leave Tarmac, your login and profile are removed; patrol records you created are kept as part of Tarmac's operational records.

5. How we protect it

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Photos are only available through short-lived, signed links to signed-in users with permission. Access is limited by role, and accounts are protected by password rules and lockout after repeated failed attempts.

6. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how it is used. Contact Tarmac Security head office (below). Some records may need to be kept where the law or a client contract requires it.

7. Children

The app is for Tarmac staff aged 18 and over. It is not intended for children.
